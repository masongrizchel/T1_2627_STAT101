# T1_2627_STAT101
Compilation of STAT101 Materials

This course deals with nature of mathematics, appreciation of its practical, intellectual, and aesthetic dimensions, and application of mathematical tools in daily life. This course deals mainly with concepts , applications, and uses of statistics as applied in the field of medicine. Topics includes collection, presentation, analysis and interpretation of quantitative data, measures of dispersion and location and inferential statistics in relation to hypothesis testing and experiments.  Statistical software such as Jamovi is integrated in the course to further strengthen the statistical knowledge of the students in dealing with their future researches.
