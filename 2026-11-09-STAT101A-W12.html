Practical Defense

Final Practical Defense Mechanics

Welcome to Week 12. This is the culmination of your biostatistics training. You will be acting as lead data analysts for a veterinary research hospital. You must execute and interpret clinical datasets live.

Rules of Engagement

  • Live Execution: You will be given a blinded VetMed dataset and a raw clinical question. You must use the Master Matrix to independently determine the correct statistical test.
  • Software Protocol: All analyses must be conducted live in Jamovi (v2.5+). You must demonstrate the correct placement of Dependent Variables, Independent Variables/Factors, and Covariates.
  • Assumption Checks: A model is invalid if assumptions are violated. You must vocally verify normality (Shapiro-Wilk), variance (Levene's), or collinearity (VIF) during your defense.
  • Time Limit: Each group has exactly 20 minutes to identify the test, execute it in Jamovi, and defend the clinical conclusion.

Defense Grading Rubric

Your final grade will be evaluated across three core competencies: Identification, Execution, and Translation.

Competency Criteria for Full Marks Weight
1. Test Selection Correctly identifies the dependent variable data type (Continuous vs. Categorical) and selects the mathematically appropriate statistical pathway from the Master Matrix. 30%
2. Jamovi Execution Properly assigns variable measurement levels (Nominal, Ordinal, Continuous), checks appropriate assumptions (normality, variance, multicollinearity), and generates the required tables/plots without instructor intervention. 30%
3. APA Translation & Clinical Conclusion Accurately interprets p-values, R², Odds Ratios, or F-statistics. Translates the raw numbers into a concise, clinically relevant APA 7th Edition summary that a non-statistician veterinarian could understand. 40%

The Clinical Scenarios

During the defense, your group will randomly draw one of the following scenario types. Prepare your analytical workflows for all of them.

Scenario Type A: Group Differences

Objective:

Determine if a new treatment is superior to a control group or an existing gold standard.

  • Likely Pathways: Independent T-Test, One-Way ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, ANCOVA.
  • Crucial Check: Verify Levene's Test for Equality of Variances before looking at the p-value.

Scenario Type B: Predictive Modeling

Objective:

Forecast a clinical outcome based on compiling patient risk factors and biomarkers.

  • Likely Pathways: Multiple Linear Regression, Binary Logistic Regression.
  • Crucial Check: Identify if the outcome is a continuous measurement (Linear) or a binary Yes/No event (Logistic). Always check VIF for multicollinearity.
