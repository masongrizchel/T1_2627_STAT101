Formulating Hypotheses (H0 vs. H1)

Null Hypothesis (H0)

Assumes NO effect, NO difference, or NO relationship.

Alternative (H1)

Assumes a significant effect, difference, or relationship exists.

Parametric vs. Non-parametric Criteria

Parametric

Requires continuous data, normal distribution, and homogeneity of variance.

Non-parametric

Used for ordinal data, skewed distributions, or small sample sizes.
