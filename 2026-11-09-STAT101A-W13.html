Final Manuscript & Synthesis

The IMRaD Manuscript Structure

Following your successful Week 12 Live Defense, your group must now translate your statistical findings into a formal scientific manuscript following the standard VetMed journal structure.

Section Breakdown

Introduction:

Establish the clinical scenario from your defense. State the research question and explicitly define your null ($H_0$) and alternative ($H_A$) hypotheses.

Methods:

Identify the variables (Dependent, Independent, Covariates) and their measurement levels. State the exact statistical tests used (e.g., "A Multiple Linear Regression was conducted using Jamovi v2.5...").

Results:

Present the raw statistical outputs. Include assumption checks (Normality, VIF, Levene's), main effect sizes ($R^2$, Odds Ratios), and exact $p$-values. Do not interpret the "why" here.

Discussion:

Translate the math into medicine. What do these results mean for a practicing veterinarian? Give a concrete clinical recommendation based on your findings.

APA 7th Edition Statistical Reporting

Proper formatting is mandatory for your final paper. Incorrectly reported statistics will result in point deductions.

The Golden Rules of APA 7th

  • Italicize Statistical Symbols: Always italicize English letters used as statistical symbols (e.g., p, t, F, r, R2). Greek letters (like $\alpha$ or $\chi^2$) are NOT italicized.
  • Leading Zeros: If a statistic cannot exceed 1.0 (like a p-value or correlation r), do not use a leading zero (e.g., write p = .034, not p = 0.034). If it can exceed 1.0 (like standard deviation or weight), use a leading zero (e.g., M = 0.85 kg).
  • Decimal Places: Report exact p-values to three decimal places (e.g., p = .042). If Jamovi outputs p < .001, write exactly that. Do not write p = .000. Report other statistics (like F or t) to two decimal places.

Writing Templates:

  • ANOVA: "There was a significant main effect of the diet protocol on weight gain, F(2, 27) = 4.52, p = .021."
  • Regression: "The overall model significantly predicted anesthesia clearance time, R2 = .45, F(3, 116) = 14.22, p < .001."

Data Visualization & Tables

Formatting Jamovi Outputs

Exporting Plots:

Right-click any scatterplot or bar chart in Jamovi, select Image > Save As... and choose PNG format. Ensure your axes are cleanly labeled in the Jamovi data tab before exporting.

APA Tables:

Do not submit raw screenshots of Jamovi tables. You must recreate them in your document following APA format: No vertical lines, and horizontal lines only at the top, bottom, and below column headers.
