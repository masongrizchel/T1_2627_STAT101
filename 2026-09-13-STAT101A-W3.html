Diagnose

Check dataset health before running complex tests.

Measures of Central Tendency

Mean

The mathematical average. Sensitive to extreme outliers.

Median

The exact middle value. Best for skewed data.

Mode

The most frequently occurring value in the dataset.

Measures of Dispersion (Spread)

Range

Difference between the highest and lowest values.

Standard Deviation

Average distance of data points from the mean.

Variance & IQR

Variance is SD squared. IQR measures the middle 50%.

Graphical Displays

Histograms

Shows frequency distribution of continuous variables. Identifies shapes.

Boxplots

Displays median, quartiles, and flags potential outliers visually.

Checking Assumptions

Normality

Is the data shaped like a bell curve? Crucial for parametric tests.

Skewness

Measures asymmetry. Left-skewed vs. Right-skewed data tails.

Shapiro-Wilk Test

Statistical test for normality. (p > 0.05 indicates normal distribution).
