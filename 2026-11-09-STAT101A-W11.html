Survival Analysis (Time-to-Event)

Used extensively in veterinary oncology and epidemiology to track how long it takes for a specific event (e.g., relapse, death, recovery) to occur.

Kaplan-Meier Curves

1 Time Variable (Continuous Days/Months) 1 Status Variable (Binary: Censored vs. Event)

VETMED EXAMPLE:

Tracking the Time to Relapse (in days) for canines treated for lymphoma. Patients who withdraw from the study or outlive the study period are marked as "Censored" (0), while those who relapse are marked as the "Event" (1).

JAMOVI EXECUTION:

  • Requires installing the jsurvival or ClinicoPath add-on module via the Jamovi Library.
  • Navigate to the newly installed Survival menu.
  • Elapsed Time: The continuous days/months column.
  • Event Status: The binary 0/1 column.

Multivariate ANOVA (MANOVA)

What happens when you have more than one continuous outcome variable that you want to test simultaneously? Instead of running multiple ANOVAs (which increases the risk of false positives/Type I errors), you run one MANOVA.

Multiple Continuous Outcomes

1 Categorical Grouping Variable (IV) 2+ Continuous Outcomes (DVs)

VETMED EXAMPLE:

Testing three different cattle diets (Supplement A, B, and C). Instead of just looking at Weight Gain, the team wants to simultaneously test both Weight Gain (kg) AND Milk Yield (Liters) as dual outcomes.

JAMOVI EXECUTION:

  • ANOVA > MANOVA (Available via base jamovi or the 'moretests' module).
  • Dependent Variables: Move ALL continuous outcomes into this box.
  • Grouping Variable: Move the categorical diet type here.
  • Interpretation: Check Pillai's Trace or Wilks' Lambda p-values. If significant, the diet affects the combined outcomes.

The Master Matrix (Final Defense Prep)

For your Weeks 12-13 Comprehensive Practical Defense, your success depends on identifying the correct statistical tool immediately upon seeing the clinical dataset. Memorize this logic tree.

The Decision Tree

If testing DIFFERENCES (Groups):

  • 2 Groups, Continuous DV: Independent Samples T-Test (Parametric) or Mann-Whitney (Non-Parametric).
  • 3+ Groups, Continuous DV: One-Way ANOVA (Parametric) or Kruskal-Wallis (Non-Parametric).
  • Same Subject Over Time: Paired T-Test (2 times) or Repeated Measures ANOVA (3+ times).
  • Categorical IV against Categorical DV: Chi-Square or Fisher's Exact (if expected counts < 5).

If testing PREDICTIONS (Relationships):

  • 1 Continuous IV → 1 Continuous DV: Simple Linear Regression / Pearson Correlation.
  • 2+ Continuous IVs → 1 Continuous DV: Multiple Linear Regression.
  • Any IV → 1 Binary Categorical DV (Yes/No): Binary Logistic Regression.
